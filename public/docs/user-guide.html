Interior Design 3D — User Guide

Design rooms in 3D: draw walls, drop in furniture, pick materials, then share or export your finished design. This guide covers everything from your first login to sharing a room.

1 Getting Started

Create an account

  1. Open the app in a browser (Google Chrome or Edge recommended).
  2. Click Register and enter your name, a valid email address, and a password (at least 8 characters with a letter and a number).
  3. You'll be logged in automatically.
No sign-up frictionNo credit card and no download — everything runs in the browser.

Logging out

Click Logout in the top-right corner of any page. You can log back in anytime with Log In.

2 Your Projects (Dashboard)

After logging in you land on the My Projects page — your home screen.

  • Create a project — type a name in the box and click New Project. You'll jump straight into the editor.
  • Open a project — click Open next to any project to continue editing it.
  • Delete a project — click Delete. You'll be asked to confirm first; deletion is permanent.

Each project shows when it was last updated. All your projects are private — only you can see them.

3 The 3D Editor

The editor screen has four parts:

AreaWhat it does
Top barProject name, save status, Save button, Logout
Toolbar (below top bar)Tools, undo/redo, furniture, snap/grid, export, share
3D viewportYour scene — click, drag, and view it in 3D
Properties panel (right side)Edit color, material, and dimensions of the selected item

Navigating the 3D view

  • Rotate the camera — click and drag anywhere on the empty ground.
  • Zoom in/out — scroll the mouse wheel.
  • Pan (move sideways) — right-click and drag.
  • Switch tools — use the toolbar buttons (Select, Move, Rotate, Scale, Wall).

Saving your work

  • The app auto-saves about 2–3 seconds after you stop making changes.
  • The status in the top bar tells you where things stand:
    • Saved (gray) — everything is up to date.
    • Unsaved changes (yellow) — changes are pending, auto-save is coming.
    • Saving... — currently writing to the server.
  • You can also click Save anytime.
TipRename your project anytime by typing in the name box in the top bar. It saves automatically.

Unsaved changes from another session?

If you closed the editor while it was mid-save, the app may offer to Restore draft when you reopen — a local copy that is newer than the saved version. Click Restore to keep your latest work, or Discard to ignore it.

4 Drawing Walls and Rooms

The Wall tool builds walls and automatically creates a floor from the outline you draw.

  1. Click the 🧱 Wall button in the toolbar.
  2. Click on the ground to place the first corner. A marker appears.
  3. Continue clicking to add more corners — each click draws a wall segment.
  4. To close the room, click back near your starting corner (it turns green), or click ✓ Complete (or press Enter).
  5. A floor is created automatically from the closed outline.

While drawing:

  • Right-click removes the last corner you placed.
  • Press Escape to cancel the wall tool entirely.

5 Adding Furniture

There are two ways to add furniture:

Quick place (toolbar)

The toolbar has one-click furniture buttons (🛋 Sofa, ⬛ Table, 💺 Chair). Click one, then click on the ground where you want it. The piece follows your cursor as a preview.

Asset Library (full catalog)

  1. Click 📦 Assets in the toolbar to open the library.
  2. Browse categories or type in the search box.
  3. Drag a piece onto the ground in the viewport — a ghost preview shows where it will land.
  4. Release the mouse to drop it.

6 Selecting and Transforming Objects

Select

Click an object with the Select tool to highlight it. Click empty space to deselect.

Move / Rotate / Scale

  1. Click an object to select it.
  2. Pick Move (✚), Rotate (⟳), or Scale (⬌) from the toolbar.
  3. A gizmo (arrows / circles / handles) appears on the object. Drag it to transform.
  4. With Snap ON, movement snaps to whole units and rotation to 15° steps for cleaner layouts.
Walls & floorsWalls and floors are edited with the Properties panel rather than the gizmo.

Delete

There is no delete button — undo (Ctrl+Z) removes the last change. To remove something, undo back past the step that added it.

8 Undo and Redo

  • Undo — Ctrl+Z or the ↩ button.
  • Redo — Ctrl+Shift+Z or the ↪ button.

10 Sharing Your Design

Anyone can view your room through a share link — no account needed.

  1. Click the 🔗 Share button in the toolbar.
  2. Click Create share link.
  3. The view-only link appears. Click Copy to copy it.
  4. Send the link to anyone — they can rotate and zoom around your design, but they can't edit it.

To stop sharing at any time, click Revoke link. The link immediately stops working.

Live designShared links contain your current design. If you keep editing, viewers see the latest saved version.

11 Exporting

Turn your design into a file you can keep or use elsewhere:

FormatWhat you get
📷 PNG A screenshot of your scene from the current camera angle. Great for emails and client proposals.
🧊 GLB A 3D model file (industry standard) you can import into other 3D software. Contains walls, floors, and furniture — but not the helper grid or gizmos.

Export buttons are disabled until your scene has at least one object.

12 Troubleshooting & Tips

SymptomFix
Nothing happens when I click furnitureMake sure you're clicking on the ground, not on another object.
The gizmo doesn't appearThe object must be selected first, and you must be on Move/Rotate/Scale.
"Project was modified elsewhere"Someone (or another tab) saved the same project. Reload the page; your latest local changes are kept in the auto-save draft.
Share link doesn't workThe link may have been revoked. Ask the owner to create a new one.
Scene looks dim or flatMove the camera around; the lighting is fixed. Try different camera angles before exporting a PNG.
Big scenes run slowlyLots of furniture can slow older computers. Keep Snap ON to keep furniture aligned and tidy.
